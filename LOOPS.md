### Vocoder bass

September’s **Cry for You** follows the full 130 BPM recording. The keyboard part comes from the performance MIDI. The bass follows the isolated bass stem’s offbeats, verse changes and breaks, with a low octave beneath the opening keyboard figure. Teaser and Main enter at their position in that same score.

Vocoder Clear, Vocoder Hold and TAL Vocoder share the written bass part. Its level follows the singer’s lower-midrange energy. The vocoder’s Dry / Wet control also sets how much bass you hear. Chord Tune sings scored bass notes as part of its harmony. TAL Vocoder uses Wet level for both the TAL return and the bass. Short carriers use their own bass figures, including the synth-bass track for **Blue**, the syncopation in **Fergalicious**, and the E turnaround in **Pesenka**. The chant pairings follow the harmony written for each pairing.

**Add song** prepares this bass voice automatically. An aligned `bass.wav` supplies the pitches and rhythm when available. Otherwise the importer measures low notes in the instrumental after subtracting the drum stem. It keeps offbeats, rests and section changes, and adds a lower octave when the recording leaves room for it. For a bassless accompaniment, a sustained major or minor triad can supply a root. Teaser, Main and section loops take their bass from the full performance and follow each cut’s measured bars and repeats.

### Around the World vocoder

Choose ATC’s **Around the World**, **Vocoder Clear**, then the **Vowel stack** preset. The eighth-note riff slides over held A minor, E minor, F, C and G voicings with a quiet E3 pedal across the first bar. A second saw four cents sharp and stronger upper harmonics give the voice a denser carrier. **Lead** mixes in the arrangement’s channel-four vocal line, transposed to the guide singer’s octave. It starts at 70%. The final chorus rises a semitone at album bar 85. Teaser, Main and Full use the same recording-relative score.

### Doin' It Right duet

Assign **Doin' It Right** to one singer’s video target and **Doin' It Right (backing)** to another’s. Press **F** on both panes. Either pane’s ♫ button supplies the shared accompaniment. Choose **Vocoder Clear** on the backing singer’s mic to sing through the Mac app’s polyphonic carrier.

Full starts at 0:56.629 on the 89 BPM desk recording. The vocoder sings one complete four-bar refrain before Panda Bear enters at 1:07.416. Both parts continue through the end at 4:13.467, then return to the same vocoder downbeat.

The vocoder sings only the first phrase of each bar on its return at 2:55.28. The full carrier in `linux-four/doin-it-right-vocoder.json` contains the note changes from the Mac song bundle’s `Doin' It Right.mid` (SHA-256 `8c3888b6c64585c1225a9611d925ac852bd018cad4154fa5be2e07fc4a8933fc`).

On a provisioned desk, the vocoder rehearsal guide isolates the repeated voice from the Mac arrangement’s two alignment references. To prepare it on another installation with those references, run the following.

```sh
src="$KARAOKE_DATA/tracks/doin-it-right/source"
part="$KARAOKE_DATA/private/linux-four/playback/Doin' It Right - Vocoder"
mkdir -p "$part"
ffmpeg -i "$src/drumless.wav" -i "$src/sung-ref.wav" \
  -filter_complex '[1:a]volume=-1[minus];[0:a][minus]amix=inputs=2:normalize=0,adelay=2360:all=1,aresample=48000,apad,atrim=end_sample=12166400' \
  -ac 1 -c:a pcm_s16le "$part/guide.wav"
```

### Cowgirl recording

The private `studio-cowgirl` cuts use Underworld’s 8:29 remastered album recording. Teaser takes the exposed sample at 0:28. Main takes the four-bar refrain at 1:29 and puts the sampled chant in the accompaniment. Full runs for 32 bars, from “I scream” through “Am, an eraser of love,” over the recorded Main refrain. The seven “everything” responses move to the foreground mic so the singer can take them. The guide and singer screen follow the foreground part in each cut. **Stripped** plays the percussion for that arrangement.

Prepare the cuts on another desk with the album stems, reviewed LRC and word timings.

```sh
PYTHONPATH="$KARAOKE_RUNTIME/linux-four" python3 linux-four/cowgirl_loops.py \
  "$KARAOKE_DATA/chants-lbd/sources/Cowgirl" "$KARAOKE_DATA/private/linux-four/songs"
```

### Starred practice recordings

The eight reviewed arrangements live in `linux-four/starred-arrangements`. The recording ID in each recipe identifies the edition used for its source bar lines and word timings. Closing Time and Bitter Sweet Symphony have the whole studio recording under Full. For the other six songs, Full plays the complete prepared Main passage.

Bitter Sweet Symphony uses the 5:57 *Urban Hymns* 2016 remaster. Its MIDI separates the sampled string chords, the high violin hook and the electric bass. The bass enters around 0:45. The high violin drops out for the first verse and returns at the refrain. Main and Teaser use that refrain at 126 BPM. The Full lyric timing follows the album-length recording, including its longer intro and outro.

To rebuild a song on a provisioned desk, pass the recipe filename without its extension and the matching source directory. The command below prepares Closing Time.

```sh
PYTHONPATH="$KARAOKE_RUNTIME/linux-four" python3 linux-four/starred_arrangements.py closing-time \
  "$KARAOKE_DATA/private/linux-four/sources/studio-closing-time" \
  "$KARAOKE_DATA/private/linux-four/songs"
```

The source directory contains `master.info.json` and the matching `instrumental.wav`, `drums.wav` and `clean-vocal.wav`. The builder stages and validates a fresh library entry before installing it.

### Hang With Me (Acoustic) – Synth Chords

The separate Robyn edition follows the 103 BPM piano performance from *Body Talk Pt. 1*. Held synth chords carry the verse’s Bm–A–D figure, the pre-chorus bass inversions and the F♯7 turnaround in the chorus. Full backing adds a quarter-note kick, rim on two and four, and closed hi-hats. Stripped plays that beat alone. The vocoder uses the same chord voicings as the backing.

To rebuild the private import, use the acoustic album recording (official artist audio ID `6Q4PL30HKH8`). Save its metadata as `album.info.json`, separate its vocal into `clean-vocal.wav`, and use LRCLIB 579528 for `acoustic.lrc`. Run the karaoke skill’s alignment and release refinement to produce `timing.json`, then run the arrangement builder with the runtime on the Python path.

```sh
PYTHONPATH="$KARAOKE_RUNTIME/linux-four" /usr/bin/python3 linux-four/hang_with_me_acoustic.py \
  "$KARAOKE_DATA/chants-lbd/sources/Hang_with_Me_Acoustic" "$KARAOKE_DATA/private/linux-four/songs"
```
